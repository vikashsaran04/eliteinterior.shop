Vikash Kumar

Co-Founder, Elite Interior | Tech Entrepreneur
Born: 23rd May 2004 (Barmer, Rajasthan)
Parents: Chanani Devi & Bhanvara Ram
Education: BSTC, Jodhpur (Lucky Institute)
Expertise: Meta Performance Marketing & UI Design

How It Started: A Childhood with Gadgets

Hello, my name is Vikas. Ever since I was a kid, I had a deep connection with mobile phones and gadgets. Right from my school days, I loved exploring how social media worked and started developing my digital skills early on. Back when I was still in school, I used to create themed Instagram pages, grow them, and sell them to others. This started generating a small side income for me, which I used to cover my school expenses and personal needs.

It wasn't that I only knew how to use phones—I was actually a very bright student too. I was the school topper with an 80% score in my 12th standard (2021) and got 68% in my 10th boards (2019) from Government Higher Secondary School, Bamarla[cite: 1]. However, as time went on, I chose not to follow traditional theoretical studies and jumped directly into the tech private sector instead.

During my high school years, my favorite project was scaling specialized Instagram niche pages focused on Rajasthani culture. It became one of the first popular pages of its time in that category, and handling it taught me the fundamentals of online community building and digital growth.

In 2019, when the pandemic hit and everyone shifted to studying from home, I got the chance to use my father’s phone regularly. I utilized that time completely to build up my online marketing knowledge. Later, when my 12th results came out good, I applied for the BSTC program, which is a popular professional course in Rajasthan[cite: 1]. I scored really well—around 450 out of 600—and got admission into the Lucky Institute of Professional Studies in Jodhpur[cite: 1].

The Jodhpur Days & Stepping into Independence

I moved from my home to Jodhpur with just a normal keypad phone. While there, I used to borrow smartphones from friends whenever possible to keep building my social media presence. Every single day brought a new task, a new problem, and a new wave of stress. But trying to solve those tech problems with my own brain helped me learn a new skill practically every single day.

I spent two years of my college life in Jodhpur, where my mind was less focused on the classroom and more on the online business world[cite: 1]. I wanted to be financially independent. Through that drive, I developed real-world skills that no institute can teach you: managing client relationships, business development, personal conversation skills, and digital marketing presentation. I realized I loved talking to new people and learning from them. I often conversed with people from countries like Canada and Morocco, which naturally made my everyday English much more confident and strong.

Within just two months of work, I bought my very first 5G Android phone—a Redmi 13C 5G. Having my own device changed everything; I no longer needed to depend on friends' phones. Soon after, I saved up enough to buy a foldable Windows laptop. Even though I thought about getting a premium iPhone first, I dropped that idea because I wanted to code and build bigger things on Windows, rather than limiting myself to just a mobile screen.

With that laptop, everything went exactly as I hoped. Without any teacher, using the help of AI tools, I deeply explored front-end web development and learned how to build high-quality websites. I started freelancing and earned well from it. At the same time, I noticed how the market worked and began running paid campaigns. At first, I was simply using the basic 'Boost Post' option on Instagram, which cost a lot and had very few features. I didn't know about the advanced Meta Ads Manager back then, but running those initial ads taught me the foundational logic of how online advertising functions.

The Breakthrough Era: Moving to Ahmedabad

When college ended, I returned home, but I didn't want to follow the local path of settling down for a minor job in small nearby towns like Dhorimana. The mindset there can sometimes limit your growth. I wanted to step outside my comfort zone to learn how large businesses run. Around that time, a great opportunity came up through our neighbor, Rajesh Prajapati, who is the founder of Ramdev Interior Private Limited[cite: 1]. My father spoke to him, and soon I packed my bags and moved to Ahmedabad to join his company[cite: 1].

My starting salary was modest, but money wasn't my motivation—I was there to learn. I never complained about the pay because the real-world experience I was gaining was priceless. Working closely with Rajesh ji was a masterclass. His vision for business completely changed how I looked at the market, taught me how to read people, and showed me how real business transactions happen on the ground.

I handled their social media marketing and completely moved them away from simple Instagram boosts to proper, data-backed Meta Facebook Ads[cite: 1]. I took control of the lead generation funnels, managed corporate client onboarding[cite: 1], and learned exactly what it takes to scale an interior business[cite: 1]. After spending a fruitful year learning the inside mechanics of the trade[cite: 1], I decided to take the entrepreneurial leap. Partnering up with my associate, we started our own premium design and execution firm, **Elite Interior**, launching it successfully on June 24, 2026.

My Business Principles

"Through my journey, I have realized that a successful business relies heavily on two groups of people. First, your workforce and on-site labor. If you treat them like family and clear their payments exactly on time, they will put their heart into the work. Second, your clients. Good marketing helps you stand out in the market, but actual sustainable business runs completely on client word-of-mouth and referrals. If your client is genuinely happy with your work, your business will never stop growing."
